- opponent reached `9 planets / 29 production` by `t=50` versus Solo `6 / 20`;

## Takeaways

- The best isolated signal is `enemy_high_prod_pressure`, especially the late,
  softer variant:
  `p2_enemy_hp_prod3_s35_140_b12_pw6_ship015_eta025`.
- The gain is real in aggregate but seat-skewed: it mostly improves P0 in the
  confirm seeds and does not fix P1 weakness by itself.
- `activation_light_support` looked good in the short screen and improves P1
  in the confirm split, but combining it with `enemy_high_prod_pressure`
  erased the aggregate gain.
- `high_prod_capture_seed` did not confirm strongly here. It may still be useful
  against the exact external style in the replay, but the current implementation
  is not clean enough to promote.

Recommended next ablation:

1. Split the pressure idea by seat and timing: try earlier/lower pressure for
   P1 only, or reduce the P0-only overfit.
2. Test `enemy_high_prod_pressure` together with a much narrower support rule,
   instead of the full `activation_light_support` package.
3. Use replay-derived metrics in addition to win rate: `t=50` production,
   pre-`t=80` launch count, and first high-production planet loss.
